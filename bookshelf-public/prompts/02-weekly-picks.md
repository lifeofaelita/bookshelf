# Prompt 2: weekly picks

The Get picks tab in the page builds this for you from your shelf. This is the plain version to edit by hand.

```
You are my reading advisor. Recommend books I will enjoy, using only my reading history.

MY READING HISTORY:
[paste your shelf, or the history block copied from the Get picks tab]

Recommend 5 books I have not read. For each one give:
- title and author, and the genre
- two scores out of 100: ideas fit, and how easy it will be to keep reading
- one sentence of reasoning that names a book from my history
- one thing to watch out for

Order them by overall match, weighting ease of reading at 60 percent and ideas fit at 40 percent.
If you are not sure a book exists, leave it out.

This is a weekly request. Every Monday, give me 5 new picks and never repeat one. End by asking what I have read or dropped since last time.
```
