# Prompt 1: build your taste profile

Use this when you do not want to type every book in by hand. Paste it into any chatbot (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini), then paste your list under it.

```
I want you to learn my reading taste. Below is a list of books I have read, with a rating out of 5 and a few words on what I thought.

For each book, work out two things:
1. Ideas: did the subject matter to me, from 1 to 5.
2. Reading: was it easy to keep reading, from 1 to 5, judged by pace, structure and voice.

Then tell me:
- the three patterns that earn a five from me
- the patterns that make me stop reading
- any book where the ideas scored high and the reading scored low (a style problem, not a topic problem)

Be specific and name books from my list. Do not recommend anything yet.

MY BOOKS:
[paste your list here]
```
