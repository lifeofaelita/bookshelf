# Prompt 4: sort my books into shelves

Handy when your import lands most books on the "Not sorted yet" shelf.

```
Sort each book below into exactly one of these shelves:
thriller, scifi, cosy, literary, memoir, narrative, psych, growth, spirit, other

Answer as a plain list in the format: Title | shelf
Do not add commentary.

MY BOOKS:
[paste titles and authors]
```

Then open each book in the page and pick its shelf.
