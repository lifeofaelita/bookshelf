Genre guide

A genre is a promise about what a book will give you: a puzzle, a feeling, a fact, a push to change something. Knowing the promises helps you see what you enjoy, and what you do not, before you buy. Every major genre is here, whether or not it is on your shelf yet, because you cannot tell you dislike a genre you have never met.

When genres mix

Most new books blend two genres. Read the label as two promises at once.

Age labels are not genres

Children's, middle grade, young adult and adult tell you who the book was written for, not what happens in it. Any of them can be a mystery, a fantasy or a love story. Young adult is often fast and easy to read, which makes it a good place to look when long, dense books wear you out.
